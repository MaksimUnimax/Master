Status-only grant; contract_blocked before key/wallet/upstream; zero paid attempts.

### B02 — ReadURL integrated implementation remaining

The route cannot be enabled by an external flag. After a safe contract exists, add the shared reserve/dispatch/tool-result/usage/settlement/reconfiguration integration and rerun it.

### B04 — Live Telegram/account-balance branch
